# The Assumption Ledger — every load-bearing design assumption, challenged

**The rule**: a load-bearing assumption enters this ledger when named, gets a
challenge (from any expert or the operator), and carries a status:
RESOLVED (with the resolution), IN-FLIGHT (the ruling pending), or OPEN (no
ruling yet). The ideation's exit requires no OPEN items at HIGH severity.
